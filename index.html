OUR STORY

A Century-Old Tradition,Reimagined

Shiva Cafe began in 1924 as a single roasting drum in the back of a spice shop. Four generations later, we still roast in small batches, still greet regulars by name, and still believe a good cup of coffee is worth slowing down for.
